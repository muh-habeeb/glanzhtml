Who We Are

Redefining Automotive Excellence

At Glanz, we believe your vehicle is a reflection of your standards. We are a premier automotive detailing and car care studio dedicated to perfection. Combining state-of-the-art technology, industry-leading products, and meticulous craftsmanship, we restore and protect the beauty of every vehicle that enters our bays.


Our mission is simple: to provide an unparalleled car care experience. Whether you drive a daily commuter or a luxury exotic, our expert technicians treat every car with the utmost respect and precision.

What We Do

Comprehensive Car Care Solutions tailored to your vehicle's specific needs.

Signature Car Wash

A meticulous, scratch-free hand wash process using pH-neutral soaps and filtered water. We ensure every crevice is cleaned, leaving a spot-free, brilliant finish.

Advanced Car Care

From paint correction and swirl removal to interior deep cleaning and leather conditioning. We restore your vehicle to its showroom condition.

Premium Accessories

Enhance your driving experience with our curated selection of high-end automotive accessories, air fresheners, and professional-grade maintenance products.

Packages & Subscriptions

Keep your car looking flawless year-round with our Glanz Membership. Enjoy priority booking, discounted services, and regular maintenance washes.
